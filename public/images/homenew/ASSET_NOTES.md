# Homenew product artwork

Three transparent product cutouts were created with the built-in ImageGen tool from Azelle's existing White Oud, Imperium and Urban Voyage photographs. Delivery copies are compressed WebP images, with shadows and gentle motion applied in CSS.

Prompt summary: extract the complete perfume bottle and black cap; retain its label artwork, brand, product name, shape and colors; remove surrounding props, banners and background; reconstruct obscured glass edges; use a transparent alpha background and dimensional studio lighting. White Oud has a slight lean, while the two supporting bottles are upright.

Original generated PNGs are preserved in the Codex generated_images directory. These assets are used only by the new homenew page.
