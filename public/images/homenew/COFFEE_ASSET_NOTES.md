# Coffee reference assets

Mode: imagegen edits derived from the user-provided coffee website screenshot. Transparent cutouts preserve the top-down camera angle; background is opaque. Original generated PNG files remain in the Codex generated_images directory. Website assets are WebP conversions.

Prompt set:
- Heart: top-down white cup and saucer, creamy latte and two cocoa hearts, handle lower right, isolated transparent background, no text or interface.
- Americano: top-down latte with delicate white leaf/rosette art and thin white rim, isolated transparent background.
- Cappuccino: top-down brown cappuccino with small white foam flowers, thin white cup rim, isolated transparent background.
- Beans: golden latte with large white leaf art and a loose ring of roasted coffee beans, top-down, transparent background.
- Background: only the reference's heavily defocused coffee-shop texture, irregular warm beige, cream, taupe, gray and coffee brown shapes; amber at middle left, creamy lower right; portrait composition, no cups, text, UI, logos, phones or foreground.

These assets are used only on /homenew. Decorative footer beans and interface icons are rendered in CSS/SVG.
