# Transparent product card images

The five product cards use transparent WebP images, prepared with the built-in ImageGen tool from the original product photographs. Originals remain available in `public/uploads`.

- `white-oud-transparent.webp`: bottle, retail box and duration badge.
- `imperium-transparent.webp`: bottle, pineapple, lavender, blueberries, cinnamon and duration badge.
- `urban-voyage-transparent.webp`: bottle, citrus, flowers, leaves, wood, amber and salt.
- `desire-edge-transparent.webp`: bottle, pineapple, apples, flowers, petals and wood.
- `conqueror-transparent.webp`: bottle and Main Accords infographic board.

Prompt set: remove only the surrounding studio background and floor to genuine alpha transparency; retain the original subjects, arrangement, proportions, bottle label artwork and readable original text; avoid added elements or redesign; keep fine clean edges with modest transparent padding in a square composition. For Conqueror, remove the black wall and reflective floor while retaining its infographic board as an object.

Delivery images are compressed to WebP with their alpha preserved. Hover no longer switches these cards to the original background-bearing photographs.
